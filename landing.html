Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need internet to use this?

No! Once the application is loaded in your browser, it works 100% offline. You can create customers, services, and orders even if your Wi-Fi goes down.

Where is my client data stored?

Your data is stored securely in your specific browser's Local Storage. It never touches our servers. This ensures ultimate privacy and compliance.

How does activation work?

When you purchase a plan, we send you a secure Activation Code via WhatsApp. You enter this code on the lock screen, and the software immediately unlocks for the duration of your plan.

What if I buy a new computer?

Simply go to the Settings page on your old computer, click "Download Backup", and load that `.json` file into the software on your new computer. It transfers instantly!
